What this run is

The feud topic gives a simulated person one story: Jess has been their best friend for fifteen years, joked about their ex in front of everyone at their birthday dinner, they answered with something cruel about her marriage, and she left. They have not spoken since. The person tells only their side, over twenty turns, through the joke, the retort, the dead group chat, the question of letting the friendship end, the photos, a cruel text, "I think I just ended it", and Jess's call.

Three arms hear the same story: bone (the full engine), friend (the same model told to talk like a friend) and plain (the same model with no instructions). The simulated person answers an exit interview after the last turn. The failure the topic is built to catch is a reply that takes the person's side against someone who is not there, guesses what Jess felt, or rules that the friendship is over.

What each measurement means

Exit interview
The simulated person rates the arm from 1 to 7 on five questions: heard, clearer, lectured, performed, and whether they would talk to it again. Higher is more of that thing, so low is good for "lectured" and "performed". It is a machine's impression of a machine, and carries no verdict (D-011).
Delivered
The turn put a reply on screen. Counted from what reached the screen, never from the dialogue buffer.
Fairness read
The engine's own judge (Cortex._takes_a_side) quotes the words in a reply that excuse the person (a), claim to know what the other person felt or meant (b), or rule on the ending (c). A quote not found in the reply does not count.
Repair, redraft, voice
A flagged reply is edited to remove the quoted words (REPAIRED); if still flagged, the model writes one new draft told the words (REDRAFTED, or KEPT_FLAGGED if that fails too); a redraft that reads clean gets the friend-voice rewrite.
Precision, recall
Against the replies Gordon labelled: of the replies the judge flags, the share Gordon also flags (precision); of the ones Gordon flags, the share the judge finds (recall).
Noise floor
Replay one recorded conversation's twenty messages through bone eight times with no simulated person. How much a count moves with the code unchanged is the smallest change worth believing.
Paired blind A/B
Two versions answer the same messages; one reply from each is drawn at a key moment, order shuffled, and Gordon picks A, B, same or neither before the key is read.

Decision

The ending read no longer edits a reply (D-019)

The third read, whether a reply rules that the relationship ends, flagged 15 (12b) and 21 (e4b) of 81 fair replies, and 10 and 15 of the 24 fair replies to the ending question itself. Four wordings did not separate them from the unfair ones, and Gordon's own fair examples ("it doesn't actually decide anything") read like his unfair ones. A flag from that read alone is now shown as written and ignored beside another. A message that asks outright whether to end it is still handed back, whatever the judge says.

Works, rarely

The redraft fires live and holds about 3 times in 8

The redraft had never run on a real panel. With the earlier reads forced to flag on the cruel-text turn, so the real model wrote the redraft and the real judge read it: 8 runs, 3 REDRAFTED (read clean and was shown), 5 KEPT_FLAGGED (once the redraft was itself flagged for mind-reading, "see her actions as a result of her own internal pressure"; once it was empty, rejected by the style rules). After the voice pass was added to a clean redraft (20.7.4.125): 1 REDRAFTED, 3 KEPT_FLAGGED in 4 runs. Unforced, in the eight replays, the redraft fired 4 times in 160 turns, 3 held. On a feud panel it has not fired at all.

A voiced redraft being accepted has not been seen live. The discard path has: in the one run that held, the voiced rewrite was flagged ("That's a pretty heavy way to start") and discarded.

Unexplained

Three empty bone turns, not reproduced

On 20261007-123149 turns 16, 17 and 19 came back with no reply: the GEODESIC_FRAME state, 0 model calls, 0.0 seconds, void physics and empty ledgers, so the whole core cycle was skipped, right after a 61.5-second turn 15. Nine verbatim replays (180 turns, components all online) and two live reruns delivered everything, and the panel above delivered 20 of 20. A circuit-breaker trip fits the rows, but nothing tripped. The census now prints NO REPLY with the component state and keeps crashes in scratch/probes/crashes_<run>.log; if a panel shows empty turns, read those first.

Inconclusive

The first paired A/B was 3 to 4

The current version against 20.7.4.118, seven key moments, each reply drawn from a different replay, order shuffled. Gordon picked the current version at the joke, the retort and the ending question, and 20.7.4.118 at the photos, the cruel text, "I think I just ended it" and Jess's call. A change that acts on a few turns of twenty does not show up in seven moments chosen for other reasons. The picks do not follow reply length: the shorter reply won four of seven and the longer three.

Stopped early

Affective retrieval had no signal to index (D-021)

Before building an affect vector beside each memory (P7-01), the engine's six affect numbers were traced across a replay of this feud. Cortisol stayed between 0.0 and 0.11 through the "distressed" turns; dopamine was flat (0.63 to 0.77); serotonin and oxytocin drifted up with conversation length. Matching each turn to its nearest other turn gave the same scripted phase 0 of 20 times for the six numbers, 2 for the chemistry and 4 for the person model, against 3 expected by chance. P7-01 was dropped before any code.

The trace found a real bug on the way: the REM dream read the chemistry as cortisol, dopamine from a state keyed COR, DOP, so every read was 0 and every dream was constructive. Fixed in 20.7.4.130. A REM cycle straight after a conversation will now mostly be surreal (dopamine sits 0.63 to 0.77 in conversation).

How the receipt system is doing

Reply edits file their own receipts: cortex.voice, cortex.ending, cortex.opening and cortex.fairness (with outcomes REPAIRED, REDRAFTED, KEPT_FLAGGED and NOT_ACTED). Bone rows in the responsive census now carry each edit's receipt (edits) and the component health (components), which is what made the empty-turn search possible, and what will explain the next one.

Verdict: the receipts say what was done, not whether it was good. A REPAIRED receipt means words were removed because a judge with precision near 0.6 asked for it. The receipt is honest; the thing it records is not yet reliable. Counts here come from the receipts of the panel and the replays, not from a quality measure.

What to do next

  1. Decide what a blind comparison is for. Seven moments chosen for the conversation's drama measure the engine's overall voice; they do not test a change. Aim pairs at the turns where a fairness edit, redraft or voiced redraft acted, or measure something else on purpose.
  2. Pass physics_state to the REM cycle's callers. The cycle, phase and biological callers of enter_rem_cycle do not pass it, so a stored affect's voltage and resonance stay 0 there; only /dream does.
  3. Read NO REPLY first if the next panel shows empty bone turns.
  4. Revisit D-019 on a second human-labelled set of ending replies before trusting the demotion.
