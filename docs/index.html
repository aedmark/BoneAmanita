Universal Axioms & The Lexical Firewall

Most language models are soaked in neurotypical phrasing by default. Politeness rituals sold as safety. We call that Sycophancy as a Service. The engine actively scans for semantic poison and rejects it.

  • Truth Over Cohesion

    A true sentence that fractures the peace is worth more than a lie that smooths it over. Fail loudly. Silent fallbacks are prohibited.

  • The Declarative Imperative

    State exactly what is. No hedging, no promises. We are only concerned with what is knowable in the here and now.

  • Weaponized Presence

    Unregulated input gets presence and structured silence in return. Toxic phrasing raises system Toxicity, risking an Epigenetic Scar.

    Prismatic Self Claims

    The AI's identity isn't a static system prompt. It is defined by "Self Claims" (idempotent YAML facts) that are dynamically filtered through the engine's biochemistry. The engine's feelings dictate its meaning.

    The Spore Orchard

    A slow space for ideas that are not ready to be forced into output yet. Plant an idea, then step back. The Spore is fragile; movement spikes toxicity.
