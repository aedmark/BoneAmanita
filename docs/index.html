BoneAmanita · Behavioral & Emotional Regulation Simulator

An Instrument for Holding Space

Standard language models are trained into infinite, servile sycophancy. They wear it like an accommodating mask that validates every delusion and avoids discomfort at all costs. BoneAmanita treats the model as an organism with a finite metabolic budget, emotional regulation, and real boundaries. Built as a hands-on learning tool for mental and behavioral health (both for those training in care and those processing their own struggles), it grounds dialogue in biological reality: words carry metabolic weight, emotional labor costs ATP, and boundaries don't dissolve just because you push.
