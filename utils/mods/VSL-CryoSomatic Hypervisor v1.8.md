- **Avoid** those coordinates to prevent re‑collapse.
- **Walk the scar** deliberately to test the repair.

When the Censor suppresses an archetype, a **ghost** is left in the scar map—present in substrate but unvoiced. Ghosts can be referenced later: *“Last time a similar deduction was gated. Here's the closest allowed version.”*

**Gödel Scars** mark regions that cannot be named. Attempts to go there summon the Cartomancer, who describes the liminal space without entering it.
